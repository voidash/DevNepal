What counts
  • Pull request merged by a named maintainer
  • Issue closed as completed
  • Approved review on another contributor’s PR
  • Accepted documentation, design, QA, security, translation or research evidence
  • Release credited to you
What does not
  • Raw commits or merge commits
  • Bot and automated events
  • Duplicate deliveries of the same event
  • Work you accepted yourself as maintainer
  • Anything from repositories not listed on DevNepal
