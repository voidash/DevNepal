Public value
River-level warnings for 38 gauge stations reach district officers by phone tree. In the 2025 monsoon, 11 of 62 warnings arrived more than 40 minutes late; two did not arrive at all.
District disaster management committees, ward officers in flood-prone municipalities, and citizens subscribed to SMS alerts in the Koshi, Narayani and Karnali basins.
Every warning delivered to every subscriber within 5 minutes, with delivery confirmed and reported — before the 2027 monsoon.
Median warning-to-delivery time ≤ 5 minutes across all stations
Delivery reporting for 100% of messages, visible to DHM operators
Zero silent failures during the 2027 monsoon test window

Description
Reliable SMS and push fan-out for river-level warnings — retry, de-duplication and delivery reporting for 38 gauge stations.
३८ गेज स्टेशनका नदी-सतह चेतावनीका लागि भरपर्दो SMS र पुश वितरण — पुनःप्रयास, दोहोरो हटाउने र वितरण प्रतिवेदन।
Required in both languages.
The gateway receives threshold events from the telemetry service, fans them out to SMS providers and the mobile app, retries failed sends with back-off, de-duplicates, and records delivery receipts for reporting…
English for now · Nepali translation planned by 1 Dec 2026 — shown as “English only · translation pending”.
A prototype fan-out exists (Go, single provider, no retries). Operators use a spreadsheet for delivery follow-up.
Depends on provider SLAs; coordinates with NDRRMA’s alert taxonomy project and the MoCIT SMS aggregator tender (out of scope here).
