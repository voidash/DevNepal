Public value
Problem

The citizen services portal handles 1.1 million requests a month, but its forms cannot be completed with a keyboard or screen reader, and error messages appear only in English.

Target users

Citizens using assistive technology, older users on low-end Android devices, and ward-office staff who complete forms on behalf of others.

Expected outcome

WCAG 2.2 AA conformance on the 14 highest-traffic services by November 2026, with Nepali and English error recovery on every form.

Success indicators
  • 0 critical axe-core issues on the 14 services
  • Form completion rate with screen readers ≥ 80%
  • All strings available in Nepali
